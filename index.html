Aegis Facility & Security: B2B Lead-Nurture Email Funnel

Designed and developed a full-funnel B2B email nurture campaign built to guide new leads from initial awareness to consultation through a structured, behavior-driven automation flow. The project focused on aligning messaging, UX structure, and lifecycle strategy to support long-term lead qualification instead of one-off promotional emails.

The campaign included strategy planning, conditional funnel mapping, MJML email development, responsive layout design, and conversion-focused CTA placement across a 5-email sequence. Each email served a specific stage in the funnel — education, solution positioning, social proof, and soft conversion — creating a cohesive system designed to improve engagement and consultation readiness.
