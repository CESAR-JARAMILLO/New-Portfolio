BizMorph Digital Agency: Building a Scalable CMS Foundation

A full website build and administration project focused on CMS customization, SEO structure, analytics setup, compliance implementation, and scalable content workflows.

Project Overview

BizMorph is a digital agency project where I managed the full website setup and administration process, from theme installation and customization to SEO optimization, analytics configuration, and content structuring. The goal was to create a scalable platform that supported marketing workflows and long-term growth.

I customized the CMS layout, created multiple service and content pages, and structured the site architecture to improve usability and performance. SEO-focused copywriting and metadata optimization ensured the site was ready for search visibility from launch.

Compliance was a key focus of the build, including implementation of privacy policy pages, terms and conditions, and cookie consent banners. Google Analytics was integrated to track performance and user behavior across the site.

Beyond development, I created blog content and supporting social media assets to help establish brand presence and support ongoing marketing efforts.

Key Highlights

  • Full CMS Setup & Customization

    Installed and configured theme template with custom layout and structured page architecture

  • SEO Optimization

    Optimized page structure, copy, and metadata to support search visibility

  • Analytics & Tracking Setup

    Configured Google Analytics to monitor user behavior and performance

  • Compliance Implementation

    Built privacy policy, terms, and cookie consent systems for regulatory compliance

  • Content & Blog Creation

    Developed blog posts and social media content aligned with brand strategy
